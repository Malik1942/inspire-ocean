# Screenshot seed pictures

The photographs and link covers that `OCEAN_SCREENSHOT_SEED=1` gives the
seeded thoughts, for the App Store, website and portfolio screens. The seed
(`Oryne/App/SeedScreenshot.swift`, DEBUG only) reads them from this folder in
the checkout the build came from, so they never ship in the app. On a device,
or from a moved checkout, the seed falls back to its drawn banners.

Generated 2026-11-02 with Figma AI (`gpt-image-2.5-flare`, 1536x1024) in the
Figma file "Oryne screenshot seed pictures", then saved as JPEG at quality 82.
The repo is in iCloud: run `brctl download design/screenshot-seed` before a
capture so the simulator isn't handed evicted files.

The link covers are editorial art for each thought, never the linked site's
own logo or branding.

| File | Thought | Prompt, in short |
|---|---|---|
| `golden-hour.jpg` | Golden hour on the walk home (Tide's stage) | A phone photo of a brick row-house street at golden hour: peach on the upper floors, slate shadow at street level, the first streetlights just on; dark pavement along the foot. |
| `sunset-study.jpg` | Sunset gradient study | A minimal sunset over a calm sea: orange at a low, flat horizon cooling through rose into violet. |
| `desk-at-6pm.jpg` | Desk at 6pm | A plain wooden desk against a wall, a peach window-light patch on the wall while the rest cools to slate; closed laptop with no logo, mug, paper and pencil, a plant. |
| `cover-interface.jpg` | Apple Design Principles (link cover) | A studio still life of frosted-glass rounded rectangles and circles in soft blue, coral and mint on warm off-white. |
| `cover-collecting.jpg` | Are.na Editorial (link cover) | Top-down found things on pale oak in a loose grid: a ginkgo leaf, a pebble, torn blue paper, a blank card, orange peel, a brass key. |

Every prompt also asked for no people, no readable text and no logos, and
for the subject to sit at the center: Tide's stage and a link card's square
thumbnail both crop about the center.
