# Oryne 2.1: App Store text

Draft for review. Sources: PRs #15 to #33 and the share and widget commits since 2.0 (released 2026-10-21).

## What's New in This Version (English)

Oryne 2.1. Quicker to catch, easier to find, and more of your Ocean on your wrist.

• Whisper with a tap. Tap the disc to start and tap Stop to keep it. Holding still works, and you can cancel a take.
• Typing opens into a card. Add pictures with +, and a link in your words becomes the thought's link.
• The Library is built for finding. Search sits with the tools at the top, results start right under it, and Summarize reads them all in one tap. Switch between Recent and Currents, filter by kind, and find Archived at the end.
• Swipe a thought left to archive or delete it, with Undo.
• Share a thought from its page. Your words and pictures leave through the share sheet, with the location taken out of photos.
• Ask understands both your languages. A question in Chinese can find a thought you wrote in English.
• Currents work across languages too. Rename them, merge them, or move thoughts into a new one.
• New in Settings: What Oryne knows about you. A few short notes drawn from your thoughts, which you can edit or delete at any time. They are made on your device while it charges.
• Redesigned widgets. Resurfacing shows the thought's picture and comes in a large size with Ask. Quick Capture now fits the Lock Screen as a rectangle or a single line.
• Apple Watch gains Today and Recent. Turn the Crown to see today's thought or your latest whispers, and play them back from your wrist.

## Promotional Text (170 characters at most; can change without a new version)

Tap to whisper, tap Stop, and it's caught. New in 2.1: a Library built for finding, sharing from any thought, and today's thought on your Apple Watch.

## Description

Your best ideas show up when you can't act on them. Walking, on the bus, mid-conversation, half-asleep. By the time you're back at a desk, the thought is gone. Notes apps were supposed to fix this, but they turn into a flat pile you never reopen, where nothing connects and nothing comes back.

Oryne is built for the catch, not the filing. Tap the disc and speak, tap Stop, and it's caught. From there, on-device intelligence does the part you never get around to: it names the thought, finds its themes, gathers it into a current with the thoughts it belongs with, and brings it back to you when it matters.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Catch it in one tap. The capture bar sits at the bottom of Tide: tap the disc and talk, then tap Stop, or hold it and let go. Tap the bar's words to type instead, add pictures with +, and a link in your words becomes the thought's link. Every thought is saved at once, with ten seconds to undo.

Catch it without opening the app. One press on the Action Button, in Control Center, or on a widget, including the Lock Screen, and Oryne is already listening. Share links, text, and images into Oryne from any app.

Catch it from your wrist. Press the Action Button on Apple Watch Ultra, tap the complication, or say "Whisper to Oryne", and speak. Your whisper reaches your iPhone on its own. Turn the Crown for Today and your recent whispers, and play them back from your wrist.

Let it organize itself. Every thought gets a title and themes on its own, and a long whisper gets a short summary. Currents hold across languages, so a thought in English and one in Chinese can share one. You never tag, file, or sort.

Start the day on Tide. The thought that came back today, what just drifted in, and your currents, in one glance. Nearby shows what sits close to it. Let it rest sends it back under.

Ask your ideas anything. One question, one grounded answer, and every answer lists the thoughts it was drawn from. Ask in either language and it finds thoughts in both. Summarize reads a whole current or a search in one tap.

Find anything in the Library. Search from the top of the page, browse by Recent or by Currents, filter by kind, and see what's related to any thought. Swipe a thought away to archive or delete it, with Undo.

Grow an idea without losing the original. Branch any thought into a Question, a Concept, a Research direction, or a Project.

Catch a link, keep the meaning. Drop in a link and Oryne reads the page on-device and summarizes what matters, so the reference becomes part of your thinking, not a dead bookmark.

Share a thought. Send its words and pictures anywhere through the share sheet, with the location taken out of photos.

Think in English or Chinese. Voice is transcribed on-device in both, and every title stays in your thought's own language.

Take it with you. Export the whole Ocean as one zip: a Markdown file per current, a full JSON backup, and your images.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

The intelligence runs on your device, not in someone else's cloud. Over time it keeps a few short notes on what your thoughts keep returning to, under What Oryne knows about you in Settings, where you can edit or delete them. Your thoughts sync across your own devices through your private iCloud and stay yours. Nothing is mined, sold, or used to train anyone's model.

Oryne is a quiet place to think, and a way to make sure the thinking adds up.
